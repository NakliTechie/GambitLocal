How to play GambitLocal

Play against Stockfish — a world-class engine running entirely in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

  • Difficulty — choose a preset (Beginner → Max) or drag the Skill slider (0–20).
  • Side — pick White ♔, Black ♚, or Random before starting a new game.
  • Time controls — choose a clock in the toolbar, or leave it on ∞ Unlimited.
  • Hover a piece to see legal moves highlighted.
  • Undo takes back your move and the engine's reply together.

Play a slow game with a friend over WhatsApp, email, or any messenger. No accounts. No app needed. The entire game lives in a URL.

  1. Switch to the Correspondence tab and click New Game.
  2. Make your first move on the board.
  3. Click 🔗 Share Position — the link is copied to your clipboard.
  4. Send the link to your opponent (WhatsApp, iMessage, email…).
  5. They open the link — the board loads at your move.
  6. They reply, click 🔗 Share Position, send the new link back.
  7. Repeat until checkmate.
